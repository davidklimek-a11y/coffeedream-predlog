Privacy policy

This site does not use cookies and does not track visitors. There is no analytics, advertising pixels, or embedded social networks.

Contact and job application forms do not send data via the site. They only prepare a message in your email app, and you decide whether to send it to office@coffeedream.rs.

The "Find the nearest" button uses your device's location only in your browser, to work out the distance to our cafés. Your location is not sent anywhere.

The Coffeedream app (bonus card) has its own privacy policy. App privacy policy.

Questions about privacy: office@coffeedream.rs.
